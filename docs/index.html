Everything a field engineer needs

Five integrated screens covering the full drive-test workflow — from live measurement to engineering report.

Live Drive Test Dashboard

Real-time RSRP gauge with RSRQ, SINR, RSSI, CQI and Timing Advance. Serving cell & 5G NR (SA/NSA) + LTE CA component monitor, dual-SIM and sampling cadence control.

Interactive Route Map

Leaflet map with 5 color modes (RSRP/RSRQ/SINR/tech/speed), 4 palettes, serving-sector lines, neighbor candidates, handover events and missed-handover detection.

Campaigns & Multi-Format Export

Searchable campaign library with batch operations and undo. Export to CSV (23-column APK schema), KML, GeoJSON and printable PDF engineering reports, plus CSV log import.

Analytics & Route Diagnostics

8-tab analytics: signal stats & CDF (P10/P50/P90), network & band breakdown, 2D heatmap, cell-site inventory, handover log, automated 0–100 route score and campaign comparison.

PWA Offline-First

Service-worker precaching, installable Web App Manifest, and offline map-tile caching — keep measuring even without connectivity.

Android Native Runtime

Capacitor bridge with a foreground service for real cellular telephony radio metrics and GNSS tracking, plus a standalone Kotlin Jetpack Compose replica.

Built on a modern, local-first stack

Data stays on your device. No cloud account, no uploads — your measurements are yours.
