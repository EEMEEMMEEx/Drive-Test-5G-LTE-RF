CORE CAPABILITIES

Built for Cellular Network Optimization

Every tool required for site acceptance, cluster optimization, coverage verification, and interference hunting.

5G NR SA/NSA & LTE CA Telemetry

Simultaneous measurement of SS-RSRP, SS-RSRQ, SS-SINR, CSI metrics, PCI, TAC, Cell ID, EARFCN/ARFCN, DL Bandwidth, and Timing Advance across primary and secondary cells.

Google Maps & Leaflet Route Inspector

Color-coded breadcrumb trails across 5 modes (RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, RAT Technology, Speed) and 4 palettes, with serving sector lines, neighbor beams, and missed-handover markers.

3D KMZ, KML, CSV & PDF Reports

Export campaigns to Google Earth 3D extruded KMZ packages, OpenGIS KML 2.2, 23-column industry CSV logs, GeoJSON features, and printable PDF engineering reports.

8-Tab Deep Analytics & CDF

Inspect P10/P50/P90 Cumulative Distribution Functions, band distribution, handover ping-pong matrices (<30s), spatial campaign delta comparisons, and 0–100 automated route scores.

Android Native Foreground Service

Uninterrupted background logging with wake-lock management, notification telemetry HUD, Dual-SIM slot switching, and OEM diagnostic dialer shortcuts (*#*#4636#*#*).

100% Local-First & Offline Ready

Zero cloud accounts or telemetry uploads. All drive-test campaigns stay on your device in Room / SQLite storage with offline map tile caching for remote rural surveys.
